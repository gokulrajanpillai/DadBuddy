Let's set up DadBuddy on this phone

This takes about two minutes, once per phone. Afterwards DadBuddy works like an app: you can log anywhere, and it syncs with the other phone when you're both on home Wi-Fi.

On iPhone, certificates and home-screen apps only install from Safari. Open in Safari and continue there.

1

Download the home certificate

It lets this phone open DadBuddy securely. It only covers baby.local and home network addresses.

Use Safari for this step. Tap the button, then tap Allow.

Download certificate
2

Install it

  1. Open Settings. Tap Profile Downloaded near the top (or go to General → VPN & Device Management).
  2. Tap DadBuddy home certificate → Install, and enter your passcode.
  3. Then go to Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings and turn on DadBuddy home certificate.
  1. Open Settings and search for CA certificate. (It's usually under Security → More security settings → Encryption & credentials → Install a certificate.)
  2. Tap CA certificate → Install anyway, and choose baby-log-ca.crt from Downloads.
  3. Android may say "your network may be monitored". That's its standard message for any added certificate.

You can remove it any time from the same settings screen.

3

Check it worked

Waiting for this phone to trust the certificate…

Open DadBuddy
4

Add it to your home screen

  1. After opening DadBuddy in Safari, tap the Share button.
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen → Add.
  3. Open DadBuddy from the home screen and tap Mum or Dad.
  1. After opening DadBuddy in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu.
  2. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Open DadBuddy from the home screen and tap Mum or Dad.

Open it at home once so it can copy the log. After that it works anywhere.
